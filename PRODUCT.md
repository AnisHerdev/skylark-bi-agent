# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Business leaders, sales directors, operations managers, and data analysts at Skylark looking for real-time visibility and actionable intelligence across sales pipelines, deal stages, client risk profiles, and work order execution schedules.

## Product Purpose
Skylark BI Agent bridges executive decision-making and operational data from monday.com boards and CRM datasets. It converts complex natural language questions into instant, accurate business metrics, risk assessments, pipeline summaries, and structured leadership reports powered by Gemini AI.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Crisp, authoritative, analytical, intelligent
- **Personality**: Sharp, trustworthy, effortless, modern
- **Emotional Goal**: Confidence and clarity in high-stakes operational and sales decisions

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, dated BI dashboards with 20 distinct rainbow colors and overwhelming wall of widgets
- Generic AI chat clones with low contrast, bland gray text, and unformatted markdown output
- Gimmicky crypto/neon cyber aesthetics with illegible glow effects

## Design Principles
1. **Earned Trust & Clarity**: Data precision comes first. Tables, metrics, and timestamps must be sharp, legible, and cleanly formatted.
2. **Modern Studio Mint Accent**: Use crisp neutrals with refined emerald and light mint accents (`#10b981`, `#059669`, `#34d399`) to signal vitality, accuracy, and freshness without visual noise.
3. **Seamless Dual Theming**: First-class light and dark modes with balanced contrast (≥4.5:1), zero FOUC, and thoughtful surface layering.
4. **Focused Workflow Canvas**: Collapsible navigation keeps the user's attention anchored on insights and instant query iteration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast ratio compliance on all text and UI elements.
- Full keyboard navigability (Enter to send, Tab index flow, Esc/shortcuts).
- Support for `prefers-reduced-motion` with non-distracting subtle transitions.
